## Content boundary

Numbas source-code licences apply to their repositories, not automatically to exams, questions, media, or textbook-derived material. The runtime documentation states that generated content remains the author's copyright. Every activity used by students must therefore have a separate provenance record for its content source, licence, attribution, modifications, AA SL competency tags, and review status.

The first baseline exploration will use newly authored or explicitly permission-cleared items for function notation, composition/inverses, algebraic rearrangement, and introductory quadratic work. Licensed Haese material remains authorized reference material only and is not placed in these forks or this repository.
