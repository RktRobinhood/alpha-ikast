## Findings

- [Reusable subject-competency and curriculum-lens model](findings/reusable-subject-competency-curriculum-lens-model.md) — defines one shared competency graph, immutable curriculum-lens releases, strict-but-nonblocking prerequisites, reusable activities, and evidence-specific cross-course mastery.
- [Cross-curricular competency and mastery patterns](findings/cross-curricular-competency-mastery-patterns.md) — compares CASE, Moodle, Khan Academy, Math Academy, and OATutor patterns for curriculum alignment, evidence, mastery, and progress.
- [Mastery policy from established learning systems](findings/mastery-policy-from-established-systems.md) — selects OATutor-style competency-level BKT as the pilot baseline, separates durable mastery from assessment readiness, and records deferred alternatives with explicit re-evaluation triggers.
- [Mathematics evidence and mastery models](findings/math-evidence-and-mastery-models.md) — compares OATutor, ASSISTments, STACK, CTAT, WeBWorK, BKT, and IRT; recommends a hybrid evidence architecture for AA SL.
- [First AA SL learning-loop slice](findings/first-learning-loop-content-comparison.md) — selects quadratic equations as the first route through curriculum, evidence, and deterministic mathematical grading, while recording content-licensing constraints.
- [Open-source learning-loop candidates](findings/open-source-learning-loop-candidates.md) — compares Numbas, Moodle/STACK, H5P, Open edX, PrairieLearn, R/exams, and PreTeXt; recommends Numbas for the first validation trial.
- [Numbas fork provenance](findings/numbas-fork-provenance.md) — records the three evaluation forks, upstream revisions, licences, and the separate student-content boundary.
- [Open-source project structures](findings/open-source-project-structures.md) — compares deployable boundaries, extension seams, self-hosting, and licenses across the candidate projects without selecting a backend.
- [Pilot component architecture](findings/pilot-component-architecture.md) — selects the OATutor and Numbas adoption boundaries, six school-owned semantic contracts, and six tracer-bullet implementation slices without choosing a backend.
